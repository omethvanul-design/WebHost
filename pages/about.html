Our Story

Founded on September 20, 2023, in Katugastota, Kandy, Ruksewana Furniture was born out of a genuine passion for unique, intentional design. We set out with a simple vision: to turn natural, raw timber into distinctive furniture that anchors a room and stands the test of time.

What began as a dedication to original craft has quickly grown into a community-first workshop where timeless woodworking techniques meet individual vision.

Rooted in Community & Craft

At Ruksewana Furniture, every piece has a human touch. We take immense pride in being an engine for local employment, providing fulfilling work and livelihood opportunities to skilled local craftspeople across our region.

Our artisans do not simply assemble parts—they shape, join, carve, and finish every custom-built creation by hand. By pairing time-honored artisanal methods with custom precision, our team transforms raw materials into heirloom-grade furniture tailored to your exact dimensions and needs.

Honest Materials, Local Heritage

The character of great furniture begins with the wood itself. We hand-select premium local and traditional timbers to ensure structural integrity and natural beauty across every project, including:

  • Hardwoods & Premium Timber: Teak, Mahogany, and Kos (Jackfruit wood)
  • Distinctive Local Species: Ginisapu, Hawarinuga, Midella, Tuna, and Albesia

Whether it is the rich, warm grain of teak or the versatile resilience of local timbers, each board is chosen to bring natural warmth, strength, and balance into your space.

Designed for Every Space

From custom dining suites for growing homes to durable, functional setups for schools, dynamic office layouts, and bespoke collaborations with interior designers, we build for the environments where life happens.

Every piece we build is custom-made with care, durability, and character—crafted to be lived with, enjoyed, and passed down.
